Introduction

Mailu Management Company is a Limassol-based company built around one idea: making life easier for our clients.

We started with residential property in Cyprus and have grown into a full-service partner for owners, residents and visitors across Cyprus, the UAE, Mauritius and Latvia. One team, one point of contact, everything handled in plain English.
